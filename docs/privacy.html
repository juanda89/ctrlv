Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 12, 2026 · Applies to Control-V for macOS and iOS (including the Share Extension and Keyboard Extension)

The short version

  • We only process text you explicitly choose to translate.
  • We never log keystrokes. The iOS keyboard sends nothing until you tap "Translate".
  • Translated text is not stored on our servers — it is processed and discarded.
  • We don't sell data or use it for advertising. No tracking.

What we collect and why

DataPurposeRetention
Text you submit for translation Producing the translation Not stored. Processed in memory and discarded. Our servers keep only the character count and model name for rate limiting.

Who processes your data

To produce translations, the text you submit passes through:

  • Supabase (our backend infrastructure, hosted in the US) — receives your request and forwards it to the language model. Does not persist your text.
  • OpenRouter / xAI (the AI language model provider) — generates the translation. Subject to their data processing terms; requests are not used for model training per their API policies.

Payments are processed by Stripe (macOS) or Apple (iOS). We never see your card number.

Transactional emails (login codes, subscription notices) are sent via Resend.

The iOS Keyboard Extension

The Control-V keyboard requires "Allow Full Access" to reach our translation service. This permission is used exclusively to send the text you explicitly choose to translate (by tapping the Translate button) to our servers. The keyboard:

  • Does not log or transmit keystrokes
  • Does not read the screen or other apps
  • Sends nothing over the network until you tap "Translate & Replace"
  • Uses collected data solely to provide the translation feature, per App Store Guideline 4.4.1

Analytics

The macOS app uses TelemetryDeck, a privacy-first analytics service that receives only anonymized feature-usage signals (e.g. "translation completed"). It never receives your text. The iOS app currently ships without analytics.

Your rights

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing info@control-v.info. Deleting the app removes all locally stored data (settings, history, session).

Changes

We'll update this page when our practices change and adjust the date above. Material changes will be announced in the app's release notes.
